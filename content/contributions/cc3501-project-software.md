## About the project

The design project for CC3501 (Embedded Systems Design and Interfacing) — a self-service drinks fridge for the Cairns
Engineering Society that tracks its own stock. A camera counts what leaves the shelf, the customer pays by coin or QR
code, and sales, temperatures and faults are logged to a dashboard. The work is split across two computers: a custom
RP2040 board runs the terminal hardware, and a Raspberry Pi 4 handles vision, card payments and networking.
